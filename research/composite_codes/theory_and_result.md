# From a winning candidate to a defensible mechanism class

## 1. Geometry belongs in measurement space

Let event-level candidates form X, a prespecified sensor/sampling operator be
H, nuisance regressors be Z, and y=HXa+Zgamma+epsilon, with known positive
definite covariance Sigma. Whiten first using W Sigma W' = I, project out WZ,
and take an orthonormal basis Q for the projected candidate span. Then

    z = Q' W y ~ N(B a, I_r),    B = Q' W H X.

Q is orthogonal to WZ. The proof also applies to covariance sigma^2 I by
retaining sigma explicitly. All comparisons occur in B, not in event-level X.
Candidate separation can disappear under convolution, downsampling or nuisance
projection. If an allowed sensor kernel is unknown, the model class must include
its possible means. A confidence construction can use the span of their union;
choosing a favorable kernel after seeing y without this enlargement invalidates
the stated construction.

Define pure means P_j={a b_j:a>=0}. With signals standardized once on the
prespecified full design grid, define bounded composite means

    M_jk(eta) = {a [w b_j + (1-w)b_k]: a>=0, eta<=w<=1-eta}.

For 0<eta<=1/2 this is the cone generated by its two endpoint directions.
The fraction is a coefficient fraction in the declared standardized units,
NOT a fraction of measured energy, neurons, or biological ACh production. Free
total amplitude includes the zero mean. Null exclusion is therefore necessary.
Positive signs are a declared restriction, not a biological finding.

## 2. Two distinct impossibility statements

**Exact alias.** If a nonzero mean belongs to a pure ray and a composite cone,
the two mechanisms induce exactly the same observation distribution. Any
procedure's pure-call probability under that pure mean equals its false-pure
probability under the aliased mixture. Pure pairwise separation does not rule
this out: x1=(1,0), x2=(0,1), x3=(1/2,1/2) have distinct rays but the equal
x1/x2 mixture equals x3. This toy example illustrates the proposition; the
substantive result is its measured six-candidate consequence below.

**No uniform protection against arbitrarily small components.** Let mu be a
pure mean and mu_epsilon=(1-epsilon)mu+epsilon nu an admissible mixture. In
whitened coordinates, KL(P_epsilon||P_0)=epsilon^2||nu-mu||^2/2. Pinsker gives
TV(P_epsilon,P_0)<=epsilon||nu-mu||/2. Therefore a procedure with pure-call
probability r under mu has false-pure probability at least
r-epsilon||nu-mu||/2 under this mixture. Taking epsilon down to zero proves
that uniform false-pure control at alpha over every positive mixture weight
forces pure-call probability at most alpha. No larger finite sample rescues
uniform power against this unbounded-near-pure class. A scientifically defined
effect floor or a claim of approximate, rather than exact, purity is required.
An amplitude/SNR floor is also necessary for any uniform power promise because
every cone approaches the null as total amplitude approaches zero.

## 3. Supported-set construction with an actual error guarantee

For fixed B and known Gaussian covariance, construct

    C_alpha(z) = {mu: ||z-mu||^2 <= sigma^2 chi2_r(1-alpha)}.

Retain every pure ray, bounded pair cone and null whose mean set intersects
C_alpha. This is one simultaneous confidence region, not a sequence of
uncorrected pairwise tests. A bounded-family pure certificate is issued only
if exactly one pure ray remains, all bounded composite cones are excluded,
and the null is excluded. The generating mechanism is never supplied to the
decision rule. If no prespecified class intersects the region, report
"none of the prespecified classes compatible" rather than selecting the least
bad one. The set contains compatible models, not posterior probabilities.

**Guarantee.** If the true mean belongs to any allowed composite class, then on
the event that C_alpha contains that mean the composite class remains supported,
so no pure certificate is issued. Consequently false-pure probability is at
most alpha, simultaneously for any mean in the whole declared continuous
mixture class. The same argument bounds wrong pure certificates when the true
mean belongs to a different pure ray. No multiplicity correction across the
15 cones is required because the proof uses one common coverage event.

The implementation also reports a separate known-variance residual test outside
the candidate span at beta=.05. Rejecting this check prevents a certificate but
does not erase the returned within-span supported set. The alpha=.05 coverage
statement concerns the within-span confidence region; combining both rejection
events as a single model-adequacy rejection would have type-I error bounded by
alpha+beta, NOT alpha. The extra gate cannot increase false-pure probability.
There is no uniform guarantee against unlisted three-way, signed, nonlinear,
history-dependent or arbitrary biological alternatives. "Pure" always means
relative to the declared null/pure/eta-bounded-pair family.

## 4. Existing evidence that motivates the result

The immutable locked-N60 stress result, produced at c71695fda83ae93407599a909097962ee3fa9e0e,
has weakest matched-pure recovery .750 but worst 50/50 mixture false-pure rate
.800 (gain+update), despite its flexible-model adequacy check. The independent
certified-design sensitivity has a worse finite minimum recovery (.665 versus
.750). That result does not invalidate its optimization certificate: it shows
that the certified asymptotic pairwise objective is not a certificate for
finite-sample mechanism identification.

The already frozen follow-up at 5dc8c9280c5d3e042ce25d926c0d856ed9776ad9 improves
targeted pairwise sensitivity but leaves six of fifteen mixture contrasts
underpowered. Its generator-specific audit labels are useful descriptive
limitations; they are not an operational rule for deciding whether an unknown
real observation arose from an enabled or disabled generating pair. The
supported-set procedure avoids using that unknown identity.

## 5. New fixed-design diagnostic, with no new observations

The local contract was written before this diagnostic was run. It uses the
same full-grid standardized six signals and locked heuristic N60 allocation,
known unit Gaussian noise, unit full-grid amplitude, eta=.25 and alpha=.05.
All 15 mixture pairs are evaluated at weights .25, .50 and .75, with 2,000
replicates each. Six pure generators, a null and the existing fixed nonlinear
probe are also evaluated. Exact repetitions by factors 4 and 16 are declared
idealized budget sensitivity, not proposed animal numbers or neural time bins.

| Ideal independent sample count | Worst pure correct certificate | Worst tested mixture false-pure | Largest pointwise 95% Wilson upper |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 60 | .0060 | .0035 | .00721 |
| 240 | .1375 | .0040 | .00787 |
| 960 | .7770 | .0030 | .00653 |

At N60 the six pure certification rates are .0250, .0115, .0585, .0060,
.0185 and .0800 in Table-1 order. The low false-pure rate is purchased by
substantial ambiguity, not by uniformly solving the identification problem.
At N60 the nearest bounded-composite squared distances from the six true pure
means are 1.0013, .9968, 2.2507, 2.1628, 2.3444 and 3.6330; every value is
smaller than the simultaneous confidence radius squared 12.5916. Thus even the
noise-free mean-centered region still intersects a composite class for every
pure candidate. Repetition expands mean separation while the rank-six
confidence threshold remains fixed; the hardest pure certificate reaches .777
at N960 in this idealized setting.

The largest Wilson bounds above are maxima of POINTWISE bounds and are not a
simultaneous confidence bound on the worst-case Monte Carlo rate. The
continuous-class .05 guarantee comes from the coverage proof, not those
intervals or the three tested weights. The original .750/.800 and new
.006/.0035 endpoints are not a controlled method comparison: the new rule,
positive coefficients, known variance and inference target differ. They should
be presented as the failure of one identification claim and the explicitly
conditional cost of a safer one.

The nonlinear probe receives no pure certificates, but this is one probe only;
it does not support arbitrary open-set adequacy. Its full-grid orthogonality
does not imply orthogonality after restricting to the locked support.

## 6. Paper-worthy claim and boundary

The contribution is not that mixtures can confuse classifiers, nor a new
general theorem about confidence sets. It is an identification boundary for
the specific ACh candidate family after measurement and nuisance handling:
pairwise candidate recovery, even with an optimized design and a flexible
adequacy gate, does not establish purity; a bounded composite alternative class
changes both the inferential target and the effective information requirement.
The theorem states what must be controlled, and the six-candidate diagnostic
quantifies the cost. Direction 1 supplies the complementary link-function
equivalence boundary; this result remains conditional on the declared linear
encoding family and must not be used to evade that boundary.

These are simulation/methodology findings, not claims that existing real ACh
data reveal mixtures or identify any Table-1 mechanism.

## Reproduction

Run `node mixture_result/test_supported_set.js`, then
`python3 mixture_result/independent_verify.py`, and finally
`node mixture_result/run_supported_set.js NEW_OUTPUT_DIRECTORY`.
The runner refuses to overwrite an existing result. The committed result and
source hashes are in `results/manifest.json`; the independent SciPy check
validates all 60 chi-square quantiles, 500 random/degenerate nonnegative cone
fits and all 240 grid information gains. Frozen earlier 15 pair residuals
agree to approximately 3e-14. Use the verifier for byte-for-byte reruns.
